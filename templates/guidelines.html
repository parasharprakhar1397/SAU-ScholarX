CLAUSE 1.0

Statutory Basis & Inter-Governmental Mandate

South Asian University was established pursuant to the Inter-Governmental Agreement signed at the 14th SAARC Summit in New Delhi by the Heads of State/Government of Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka.

Under Section 12 of the University Statute, the Standing Committee on Scholarships and Financial Assistance (SCSFA) is empowered to disburse fellowships, fee remissions, and emergency subsidies to enrolled students to foster equitable academic participation across all eight member nations.

CLAUSE 2.0

Currency & Locked Exchange Rate Ordinance

Resolution of the SAU Finance Committee (FC/2026/03):

To protect international scholars from currency volatility during the academic session, all tuition fees, scholarships, research grants, and monthly maintenance stipends for Session 2026–2027 are pegged to the United States Dollar (USD) and locked at an official statutory exchange rate of ₹91.00 INR per USD. Disbursals through Indian banking channels shall be calculated exclusively at this locked benchmark.

CLAUSE 7.0

Appeals & Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Any applicant aggrieved by the provisional decision of the Scrutiny Committee may lodge an appeal addressed to the Chairperson, Standing Committee on Scholarships & Financial Aid within 7 calendar days of the publication of provisional lists. Appeals must be submitted through the Financial Aid Cell (Room 204, Administrative Block, Gaushala Road, Maidan Garhi, New Delhi – 110068) or via email at financial-aid@sau.int.
