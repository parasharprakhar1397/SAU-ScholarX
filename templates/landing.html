Procedural Workflow

Official 5-Tier Scrutiny & Sanction Process

Adjudication timeline governed by the SAU Standing Committee on Scholarships & Financial Aid (SCSFA).

Stage 01

Online Application

Student registers with SAU Roll Number and submits CGPA on a 10.00 scale with prescribed Annexure affidavits.

Stage 02

Faculty Scrutiny

Departmental Scrutiny Committee verifies entrance rankings, SGPA/CGPA marksheets, and 75% minimum attendance.

Stage 03

Revenue Verification

For need-based schemes (FSS), income certificates from competent national revenue authorities are validated.

Stage 04

SCSFA Committee Board

The Central Standing Committee reviews composite merit-need scores and recommends sanction quota lists.

Stage 05

Sanction & Disbursal

Official Gazette notification published; fee waiver credited and monthly stipends disbursed in USD / INR at ₹91/USD.
