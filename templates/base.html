SOUTH ASIAN UNIVERSITY

An International University Established by the Member States of SAARC

South Asian University was established by an Inter-Governmental Agreement among the eight member states of SAARC (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka). Degrees awarded by SAU are recognized by all eight member nations.
